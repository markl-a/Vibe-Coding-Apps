# HealthTech

This directory contains health technology and medical informatics projects.

## Categories

### telemedicine
Telemedicine and remote healthcare platforms.

### health-monitoring
Health monitoring systems and wearable integrations.

### medical-imaging
Medical imaging analysis and diagnostic tools.

### patient-management
Patient management and electronic health record systems.

### fitness-wellness
Fitness tracking and wellness applications.

## Technology Stack
- **Standards**: HL7 FHIR, DICOM, ICD-10
- **Security**: HIPAA compliance, encryption
- **AI**: Medical imaging ML, predictive analytics
- **Platforms**: React Native, Flutter, Web
